Architecture of Human-Machine Feedback

When interactive design avoids superfluous decoration and adheres to kinematic realism, the user develops immediate muscle memory. The follower ring operates via a single linear interpolation step (lerp factor 0.20), eliminating artificial rubber-band latency while ensuring fluid momentum.
